## Design Context
### Users
A desktop user seeking immediate assistance with visible screen content and live audio while working. The app lives as a compact overlay.
### Brand Personality
Quiet, direct, focused. Useful answers take precedence over dashboards or marketing surfaces.
### Aesthetic Direction
Native Mac floating panel. Compact dark presentation as proposed; platform-native controls and readable typography. Capture state and protection limitations remain visible.
### Design Principles
- Keep the current work visible around the assistant.
- Make screen/audio capture deliberate and observable.
- Put answers ahead of configuration.
- Report actual capability and failure states.

### Updated direction
Flagship polish requested. Use Radix Themes controls embedded in the native panel, with bundled Plus Jakarta Sans as explicitly requested by the user. Quiet charcoal surfaces, soft mint accents, precise compact spacing, separate settings pages, and readable Markdown answers.
